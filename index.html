AN EVENING OF DISTINCTION

About the Evening

A celebration of leadership, legacy and new beginnings.

Dowerglen High School warmly welcomes you to an evening dedicated to recognising leadership, celebrating service and honouring the legacy of our prefect body.

The evening will be hosted by our Master of Ceremonies, Mr Zakwe, who will guide guests through this special occasion.

We are also honoured to welcome a special guest, a former Head Girl and proud alumna of Dowerglen High School, whose presence creates a meaningful connection between past and present leadership.

One of the highlights of the evening will be the official announcement of the 2027 Prefect Executive, marking a new chapter of responsibility, commitment and service.

"Elegance is not simply what we wear; it is how we carry ourselves."
